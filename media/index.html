Media

Accolades & Media Attention

Cheryl has been named as a Woman Worth Watching, and her award-winning programs have been recognized in publications such as Psychology Today, The Academic Leader, and The Higher Education Workplace Magazine.

As a trainer, consultant and executive coach, she has served a host of prominent clients such as Harvard University, Federal Reserve Bank, JPMorgan Chase, Dropbox, March of Dimes, Cornell Medical Center, National Center for Civil & Human Rights, Barclays, Phillips Exeter Academy, Sunshine & Sachs, Columbia University, Jack and Jill of America, Inc., YWCA of Greater Atlanta, CARE USA, Iconic Womanhood, 100 Black Men of America, Takeda Pharmaceuticals, and Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport.

Media Coverage

  • The Wall Street Journal/MSN News/The Australian/Barron’s – “Email Enigma: When the Boss’s Reply is Cryptic” – National article discussing how Cofield successfully navigates the complexities of email communication with her supervisor.
  • Higher Education Workplace Magazine – “A Roadmap for ERGs with HR at the Wheel” – Story recognizing the thought leadership, and methodologies utilized by Cofield in creating innovative employee programming Groups at Georgia Tech. https://www.cupahr.org/wp-content/uploads/HEWorkplace-Vol5No2-2.pdf
  • Higher Education Workplace Blog – “Celebrating Quiet Brilliance” – Cofield recognized as pioneering thought leader for reminding the world of the broad spectrum of individual traits (beyond race and gender) that deserve to be recognized and celebrated in the workplace.
  • The Introvert Energizer – “Remembering Introverts” – Georgia Tech’s pioneering employee program for Introverts acknowledged as Cofield’s brain child, and referred to as “dazzling” by author and national blogger, Nancy Okerlund.
  • The Academic Leader – “The Academic Leader’s Role in Promoting Workplace Excellence” – Cofield outlines self-reflective approaches that enable leaders to reduce behaviors that inadvertently detract from supportive work environments.
  • Higher Education Workplace Magazine – “Creating the Leaders of the Future: Integrating Diversity and Leadership Development”- Cofield’s D&I program referred to as “sophisticated” and a “promising practice” in article focused on the interconnectivity between D&I initiatives, leadership development, and broader people strategies.
  • Leadership 500 Award from HR.com – Georgia Tech recognized as national thought leader for conceptualizing and implementing employee group for introverts.
