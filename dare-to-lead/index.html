What is Dare to Lead™?

Dare to Lead™ is not your average leadership training. It’s a globally acclaimed courage-building curriculum based on two decades of research by New York Times bestselling author, Dr. Brené Brown. It’s experiential. It’s evidence-based. And it’s built to spark the kind of cultural change that lasts.

Dare to Lead™ programs are designed create brave leaders and build courageous cultures based on the 4 skillsets of courage.

Rumbling with Vulnerability

Dispel vulnerability myths; enhance workplace connection; learn strategies for shame resilience; and overcome common barriers to empathy and self-compassion.

Living into Values

Operationalize behaviors that support integrity and values-alignment; and replace destabilizing “power over” mindsets with behaviors that create connection and expand possibilities.

B.R.A.V.I.N.G. Trust

Learn behavioral elements of trust to cultivate workplace connection, strategies for setting boundaries, and tools for navigating tough feedback conversations.

Learning to Rise (Resilience)

Reset after setbacks, failures, disappointment; interrogate “made up” stories that shape thinking and behavior; and learn language to navigate tough conversations with courage and clarity.

The Dare to Lead™ program explores the tenants of courageous leadership—where vulnerability is not weakness, but the birthplace of innovation, accountability, deep connection, and lasting culture transformation.

Delivered virtually or in person, this experience is for individuals, teams, and organizations ready to lead with heart, hold space for tough conversations, and lead differently by centering courage – the real currency of workplace well-being.

This is more than leadership theory—it’s practical, personal, and powerful. With Cheryl’s guidance, you’ll work through:

Heart of Daring Leadership

  • Radical self-awareness and emotional literacy
  • Answer your “call to courage” and examine ways leaders “armor up” in the face of fear

Rumbling with Vulnerability

  • Dispel myths around vulnerability
  • Recognize and regulate shame triggers and patterned responses to shame
  • Learn how scarcity, perfectionism, and comparison derail workplace connection

Living into Values

  • Identify top 2 core values and concrete behaviors that support values and integrity alignment

B.R.A.V.I.N.G. Trust

  • Skills to operationalize the 7 behavioral elements of trust
  • Strategies for building trust incrementally over time
  • Strategies for setting boundaries and navigating tough feedback conversations

Learning to Rise

  • Examine stories we make up to decrease reliance on narratives that shape workplace thinking and behaviors.
