Meet Your Facilitator

Cheryl Cofield | Cultural Strategist | Certified Dare to Lead™ Facilitator

Cheryl, a 25-year veteran attorney, HR, LOD, and master facilitator and storyteller, brings lived experience, deep wisdom, street smarts, and transformative facilitation to this journey. With a voice that invites reflection, and a calm essence that fosters belonging, Cheryl creates safe containers for individuals and organizations to evolve beyond performative leadership toward the real work of courage-building, trust, authenticity, and self-mastery.

Rumbling with Vulnerability

Dispel vulnerability myths; enhance workplace connection; learn strategies for shame resilience; and overcome common barriers to empathy and self-compassion.

Living into Values

Operationalize behaviors that support integrity and values-alignment; and replace destabilizing “power over” mindsets with behaviors that create connection and expand possibilities.

B.R.A.V.I.N.G. Trust

Learn behavioral elements of trust to cultivate workplace connection, strategies for setting boundaries, and tools for navigating tough feedback conversations.

Learning to Rise (Resilience)

Reset after setbacks, failures, disappointment; interrogate “made up” stories that shape thinking and behavior; and learn language to navigate tough conversations with courage and clarity.

Why This Matters Now

We’re living in complex times. Teams are tired. Trust is frayed. And the old models of leadership won’t get us where we need to go. This program gives you the tools, skills and strategies as well as the language and clarity to lead with purpose—even when it’s hard. Especially when it’s hard.
